1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the MoMo SMS messaging program. MoMo is currently a personal project used for low-volume conversational and personal productivity communications between Zlatin Gorov and contacts who have explicitly agreed to receive SMS messages.

2. Information collected

The MoMo SMS program may process information such as:

Information is used only as needed to:

These providers process information only as needed to provide the relevant service and are not authorized by Zlatin Gorov to use SMS opt-in information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

6. Retention

MoMo keeps messaging and operational information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to technical, security, and legal requirements. Telecommunications providers may retain records under their own policies and legal obligations.
